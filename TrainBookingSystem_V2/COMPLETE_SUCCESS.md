## 📧 **GMAIL SETUP (For Tickets):**

To enable automatic ticket emailing:

1. Visit: https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Enable **2-Step Verification**
3. Generate **App Password** for "Mail"
4. Open **Settings ⚙️** tab in the app
5. Enter Gmail + App Password
6. Click **Save**
7. ✅ Done! Tickets email automatically

**Note:** If you don't configure email, the app still works perfectly! You just won't get email tickets.

## 🎯 **HOW TO USE:**

### **First Time (Register):**
1. See the welcome screen? Click **"Register Now"**
2. Fill in:
   - Full Name
   - Gmail Address (must be @gmail.com)
   - Password (min 6 characters)
   - Phone Number
3. Click **"Register & Send Code"**
4. **Check your Gmail** for 6-digit verification code
5. Enter code and click **"Verify Email"**
6. ✅ Registered! Now you can login

### **Every Time After (Login):**
1. Click **"Login Now"**
2. Enter your **Gmail address**
3. Click **"Send Code"** (no password needed!)
4. **Check your Gmail** for one-time login code
5. Enter the code
6. Click **"Login"**
7. ✅ You're in!

### **Book a Ticket:**
1. Go to **"Quick Booking"** tab
2. Select train from dropdown
3. Choose date (tomorrow pre-filled)
4. Select coach number
5. **Click any blue seat** on the interactive map
   - Seat turns green when selected
6. Your name & phone auto-fill
7. Click **"Book Ticket"**
8. 🎉 Success! Option to email ticket

## 🐛 **TROUBLESHOOTING:**

### **GUI not appearing?**
→ Check taskbar - it might be minimized  
→ Look for "Bangladesh Railway" window  
→ Try: `python main_v2.py`

### **"Email not configured" message?**
→ That's okay! App works without email  
→ To enable: Configure in Settings ⚙️ tab  
→ You only need it for automatic ticket delivery

### **Can't click seats?**
→ Red seats = already booked (can't select)  
→ Blue seats = available (clickable)  
→ Green seats = your selection

### **Code not working?**
→ Codes expire in 10 minutes  
→ Click "Send Code" again for a new one  
→ Check your Gmail spam folder
